About Wigley's Wheels

Curated Classic Cars & Motorcycles

Wigley's Wheels is built on one principle: every vehicle we offer should be exceptional. Each car and motorcycle is personally selected, thoroughly vetted, and properly recommissioned — inspected down to every nut and bolt — before it earns a place in the collection. What you're browsing is a curated selection, not a general used lot, chosen with a genuine passion for vintage vehicles and the stories they carry. Our goal is simple: to bring your motoring dreams to life.

🛠

Properly Recommissioned

Each vehicle is inspected thoroughly, we want to make sure that the finished product we list online is as close to original factory spec as possible. Our high standards ensure that you the customer will receive a top of the line, vintage motor that can be used day in, day out.

🔍

Professionally Sourced

Our vehicles are carefully sourced both locally and internationally to ensure we provide a vast array of quality motors for our customers to choose from. Each vehicle is inspected in detail by our experienced professionals so that we can ensure they meet our high standards.
